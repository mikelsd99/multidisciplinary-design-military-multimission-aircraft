# Multidisciplinary Design of a Military Multi-Mission Aircraft (Team Project)

## Project Overview
This project involved the complete design of a military aircraft built for missions such as surveillance, cargo transport, and medical evacuation. I worked as part of a multidisciplinary team where my primary responsibility was the **CAD Design and Systems Integration using CATIA V5**.

The final model was developed through several iterations to ensure the aircraft met the performance requirements set by the aerodynamics and structures departments.

## My Contributions (CAD & Design)

### 1. 3D Modeling (CATIA V5)
* **Airframe Design:** I was responsible for modeling the fuselage, wing, and stabilizers from the beginning.
* **System Integration:** I integrated the landing gear and mission-specific systems into the 3D model.
* **Internal Configuration:** I designed the internal layout for different cabin types (cargo, personnel, and medical) based on mission requirements.

### 2. Design in Context & Collaboration
* **Iterative Design:** I updated the CAD models based on feedback and data from the aerodynamics and propulsion teams.
* **Weight & Balance Support:** I used the 3D model to provide volume and mass data to my colleagues for their structural and center-of-gravity calculations.
* **Manufacturing Limits:** I ensured that the parts designed in CATIA followed standard manufacturing constraints.


## Team Collaboration
While I managed the CAD and 3D integration, my colleagues were responsible for:
* **Aerodynamics:** 2D/3D analysis and airfoil selection.
* **Performance:** Propulsion selection and mission profiling.
* **Structures:** Mass breakdown, load analysis, and CG envelope.
